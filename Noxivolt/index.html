01 / The Frequency

We don’t just throw parties. We create worlds.

Noxivolt is a meeting point for sound explorers, visual thinkers, and people who find their rhythm after dark. From intimate listening sessions to high-energy dance floors, every gathering is shaped with intention — from the first pulse to the final track.

Built on sound.

Made for the moment.

06 / Who We Are

This is Noxivolt.

We are an independent electronic culture collective exploring the space between music, movement, and visual art. We build nights with care, welcome curious minds, and make room for sounds that deserve to be heard.

No script. No static. Just signal.

Our promise

The night is better when everyone belongs.

Come as you are. Move how you feel. Respect the room, the artists, and each other. Noxivolt is for people who show up with curiosity and leave a little more connected.
